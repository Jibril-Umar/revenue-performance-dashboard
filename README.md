# 🛒 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Dashboard Overview
An end-to-end sales performance dashboard analyzing **$32.87M in total revenue** and **1.64M in total profit** across global regions, product categories, and payment preferences.

![Amazon Sales Analysis](amazon-sales-analysis.png)

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Performance Highlights
* **Total Revenue:** $32.87M
* **Total Profit:** $1.64M
* **Average Rating:** 3.00 Stars
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** Beauty ($5.6M), Books ($5.5M), and Fashion ($5.5M) lead total sales.
* **Geographic Reach:** Covers Middle East, North America, Asia, and Europe markets.
* **Payment Distribution:** Balanced across Wallets, UPI, Cash on Delivery, Credit Cards, and Debit Cards (~20% each).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **Power BI:** Custom dark-mode visual layout, dynamic category slicers, KPI card design, and trend modeling.
* **SQL:** Data extraction, cohort aggregation, and metric validation.
* **Data Storytelling:** Translating raw e-commerce metrics into operational business insights.
